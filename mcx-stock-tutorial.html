3 Initial System Setup

Before recording transactions, set up your master data. We recommend doing these in order:

Warehouses→ Tax Rates→ Categories→ Products→ Suppliers→ Customers

🏢 Warehouses

Go to Inventory → Warehouses. Each warehouse has a name, code, and location. You must have at least one active warehouse before creating products or orders.

🧾 Tax Rates

Go to Finance → Tax Rates. Create tax rates (e.g., SST 8%) that will auto-apply to sales and purchase orders.

🏭 Suppliers

Go to Purchasing → Suppliers. Add supplier names, contact details, and payment terms. You need at least one supplier to create purchase orders.

👥 Customers

Go to Sales → Customers. Add customer contact info, credit limits, and payment terms. You need at least one customer to create sales orders or quotations.

4 Products & Categories

Products are the heart of your inventory. Go to Inventory → Products.

➕ Adding a Product

1

Click + Add Product.

2

Fill in the details:

  • Name — product name (e.g., "Wireless Mouse")
  • SKU — your internal stock code (e.g., "WM-001")
  • Barcode — EAN/UPC code (optional, used for barcode scanning)
  • Category — group products (create categories from the Products page)
  • Unit — base unit (pcs, kg, box, etc.)
  • Cost Price — what you pay the supplier
  • Selling Price — what you charge customers
  • Reorder Level — minimum stock before reorder is suggested
  • Expiry Date — for perishable items (optional)
  • Tax Rate — SST rate applicable
3

Click Save. The product now appears in the list with its current stock level.

Set Reorder Levels!The Reorder Level field powers the automatic low-stock alerts and Auto-PO feature. Leave it at 0 if you don't want alerts for that product.

🏷️ Categories

From the Products page, click + Category to create product categories. These are used for filtering, reporting, and the category pie chart.

🖨️ Barcode Labels

Click the Print Label button on any product to print a barcode sticker for your shelves or products.

5 Purchase Workflow

Purchasing brings stock into your warehouse. The flow is:

Create PO→ Receive via GRN→ Stock Updated→ Record Payment (AP)

🛒 Creating a Purchase Order

1

Go to Purchasing → Purchase Orders. Click + New PO.

2

Select the Supplier and Warehouse where goods will be received.

3

Add line items: pick a Product, enter Quantity and Unit Cost. Click + Add Item for more lines.

4

Select Tax Rate and Discount if applicable. The total (subtotal + tax − discount) calculates automatically.

5

Click Save. The PO is created with status Pending.

Auto-PO from Low StockOn the Stock Levels page, click Create Auto-PO to instantly generate a purchase order for all products below their reorder level. The system suggests quantities and uses the product's cost price.

📥 Receiving Goods (GRN — Goods Received Note)

1

When goods arrive, go to Sales → Delivery & GRN and open the GRN tab.

2

Select the purchase order you are receiving. The line items appear.

3

Enter the quantity received for each line (can be partial). Enter Batch Number and Expiry Date if applicable.

4

Click Receive. Stock levels increase automatically, and the PO status updates to Received.

💳 Paying Suppliers (Accounts Payable)

Go to Finance → AR & AP, open the Accounts Payable tab. Each received PO creates a bill. Record payments against each bill to track outstanding amounts.

6 Sales Workflow

Selling takes stock out of your warehouse. The flow is:

Quotation→ Sales Order→ Reserve Stock→ Delivery Note→ Invoice & Payment (AR)

📝 Quotations

Go to Sales → Quotations. Create a price quote for a customer. Quotations can be converted directly into a sales order with one click.

💰 Sales Orders

1

Go to Sales → Sales Orders. Click + New Sales Order.

2

Select Customer and Warehouse.

3

Add products, quantities, and unit prices. Tax and discount calculate automatically.

4

Set Payment Terms and Due Date. Click Save.

🔒 Reserving Stock

On a sales order, click Reserve to lock stock for that order. Reserved stock cannot be used by other orders. Click Fulfill to release stock and mark the order as delivered.

🚚 Delivery Notes

Go to Sales → Delivery & GRN, open the Delivery Notes tab. Create a delivery note from a fulfilled sales order to document goods leaving the warehouse. Click Print for a delivery receipt.

🧾 Invoices & Payment (Accounts Receivable)

Go to Finance → AR & AP, Accounts Receivable tab. Each fulfilled sales order creates an invoice. Record customer payments. Overdue invoices are flagged on the dashboard.

Print DocumentsUse the Print button on any sales order, quotation, or PO to generate a professional PDF-ready document.

7 Stock Management

📊 Stock Levels

Go to Inventory → Stock Levels. This shows live quantity of every product per warehouse.

  • Adjust — manually increase or decrease stock (for damages, write-offs, or corrections)
  • Transfer — move stock between warehouses
  • Create Auto-PO — generate a purchase order for all low-stock items in one click
Reorder Suggestions TableAt the top of the Stock Levels page, a highlighted table lists every product below its reorder level, showing the suggested reorder quantity and estimated cost. Use this to decide what to reorder.

🔄 Movements

Go to Inventory → Movements. This is the audit trail of every stock change (in, out, transfer, adjustment) with timestamps and the user who made the change.

✅ Stocktakes

Go to Inventory → Stocktakes. Use stocktakes for periodic physical counting:

1

Create a new stocktake for a warehouse.

2

Enter the counted quantity for each product.

3

Approve the stocktake — the system automatically adjusts stock to match the counted quantities and records variances.

📷 Barcode Scan

Go to Inventory → Barcode Scan. Use a barcode scanner (or type the barcode) to quickly look up products, check stock, and find pricing — ideal for warehouse floor use.

8 Batches, Expiry & FIFO

Go to Inventory → Batches & Serials.

➕ Adding a Batch

Each batch tracks a specific production lot with its quantity, warehouse, batch number, serial number (optional), and expiry date. Batches are typically created automatically when receiving goods via GRN, but you can add them manually too.

⚠️ Expiry Alert Summary

At the top of the Batches page, four cards show:

🔴
Expired
Batches past expiry date
🟠
≤ 30 Days
Expiring within 30 days
🟡
31–90 Days
Expiring within 90 days
🟢
Healthy
More than 90 days left

🔄 FIFO Issue Suggestions

The system automatically recommends which batch to issue first (earliest expiry date). The FIFO table shows:

  • Product — which product needs attention
  • Batch to Use First — the batch number with the earliest expiry
  • Expiry Date — with days remaining
  • Priority — URGENT (expired), HIGH (≤30 days), or MEDIUM (31–90 days)
Save Money with FIFOAlways issue the batch suggested by the FIFO table first. This minimizes expiry write-offs and directly saves the business money.

12 Tips & Best Practices

1. Set Up Master Data FirstAlways create warehouses, tax rates, and at least one supplier and customer before recording transactions.
2. Use Reorder LevelsSet a realistic reorder level on every product. The Auto-PO feature will then automatically suggest what to buy and how much — preventing stockouts.
3. Follow FIFO for ExpiryAlways issue the batch recommended by the FIFO Issue Suggestions table first to minimize expiry losses.
4. Record Payments PromptlyRecord customer and supplier payments as soon as they happen so your AR/AP dashboard stays accurate and overdue alerts are meaningful.
5. Do Regular StocktakesRun stocktakes monthly to catch variances early. The system auto-adjusts stock and logs all differences.
6. Use the Reports WeeklyCheck the slow-moving and dead stock reports weekly to take action on stagnant inventory before it costs you money.

🚀 Common Day-in-the-Life Workflow

  1. Check the Dashboard for overdue AR and low-stock alerts.
  2. Review Reorder Suggestions on the Stock Levels page → click Create Auto-PO.
  3. Process any new Sales Orders → reserve stock → create delivery notes.
  4. Record incoming goods via GRN when deliveries arrive.
  5. Record payments in AR & AP.
  6. Check FIFO suggestions and expiry alerts on the Batches page.
