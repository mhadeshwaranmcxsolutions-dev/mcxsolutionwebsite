4 Initial Setup (Departments & Positions)

Before adding employees, set up your company structure.

Departments→ Positions→ Employees

🏢 Departments

1

Go to Settings → Departments.

2

Click + Add Department and enter the department name (e.g., "Sales", "Engineering", "Finance").

3

Click Save.

💼 Positions

1

Go to Settings → Positions.

2

Click + Add Position, enter the job title, and select which department it belongs to.

3

Click Save.

ExampleCreate department "Engineering", then positions "Software Engineer", "Senior Engineer", "Engineering Manager" under it.

5 Employee Management

Go to Employees in the sidebar. This is your central employee directory.

➕ Adding an Employee

1

Click + Add Employee.

2

Fill in the details:

  • First Name & Last Name — employee's full name
  • Email — work email (used for login)
  • Phone — contact number
  • Department — select from the list
  • Position — select job title
  • Role — admin / hr / manager / employee
  • Status — active / inactive / on_leave
  • Hire Date — date of joining
  • Salary — monthly basic salary (in RM)
3

Click Save. The employee now appears in the directory.

🔍 Managing Employees

  • Search & Filter — find employees by name, department, or status
  • Edit — update employee details, role, salary, or status
  • Status — mark as inactive when an employee leaves
Set the Role CorrectlyThe employee's role determines what they can see and do. Only assign "admin" to trusted personnel, as they can change system settings and delete records.

6 Attendance & Shifts

⏰ Attendance

Go to Attendance. This tracks daily check-in and check-out for every employee.

  • Clock In / Clock Out — employees mark their attendance daily
  • Status — Present, Absent, Late, or On Leave
  • View by Date — filter attendance for any specific day
  • Monthly Summary — see total present, absent, and late days per employee

🕐 Shifts

Go to Shifts. Define work schedules for your team.

1

Click + Add Shift.

2

Enter shift name (e.g., "Morning Shift"), start time, end time, and working days.

3

Assign the shift to an employee. Click Save.

Late DetectionThe system compares clock-in time against the assigned shift's start time to automatically mark employees as Late.

7 Leave Management

🌴 Applying for Leave (Employee)

1

Go to Leave. Click + Apply Leave.

2

Select Leave Type: Annual, Sick, Personal, or Unpaid.

3

Choose Start Date and End Date. The system auto-calculates the number of days.

4

Enter a Reason. Click Submit. Status becomes Pending.

✅ Approving Leave (Manager / HR)

  • Go to Leave and view the Pending Requests.
  • Review the request, check the employee's leave balance.
  • Click Approve or Reject.
  • Approved leave automatically updates the employee's leave balance.

📊 Leave Balance

Each employee has an annual leave balance. The system tracks:

  • Annual — total days vs. days used
  • Sick — total days vs. days used
  • Personal — total days vs. days used
Don't Approve Without Checking BalanceAlways check the employee's remaining leave balance before approving, especially for annual and personal leave.

📝 Processing Payroll for a Month

1

Go to Payroll. Select the Month and Year.

2

Click + Generate Payroll to create payroll records for all active employees.

3

For each employee, adjust Overtime and add any Bonus/Allowance or Deductions.

4

The Net Salary recalculates automatically.

5

Click Mark as Paid once salaries are disbursed. Status changes from Pending to Paid.

Statutory DeductionsThe system supports EPF, SOCSO, and PCB deductions. Add them as deduction line items in the employee's payroll record.

9 Recruitment

Go to Recruitment. Manage job postings and track applicants through the hiring pipeline.

Job Posting→ Applicants→ Screening→ Interview→ Offer→ Hired

📢 Creating a Job Posting

1

Go to the Job Postings tab. Click + New Job.

2

Enter job Title, Department, Description, and Requirements.

3

Choose Employment Type: full-time, part-time, contract, or internship.

4

Set Posted Date and Closing Date. Set status to Open.

👤 Tracking Applicants

  • Go to the Applicants tab to see all candidates.
  • Each applicant has a Status: Applied → Screening → Interview → Offer → Hired or Rejected.
  • Rate applicants with a star rating and add notes.
  • Move applicants through the pipeline by updating their status.
Convert to EmployeeWhen an applicant is marked as Hired, use their details to create a new employee record in the Employees module.

10 Onboarding

Go to Onboarding. This module ensures new hires complete all required setup tasks.

📋 Onboarding Tasks

Each new employee gets a checklist of onboarding tasks. For example:

  • Sign employment contract
  • Set up company email & accounts
  • Receive laptop & access card (from Assets)
  • Complete company policy briefing
  • Introduction to team

➕ Creating Onboarding Tasks

1

Click + Add Task.

2

Select the Employee, enter Task Title and optional Description.

3

Set a Due Date and assign an owner.

4

Track status: Pending → In Progress → Completed.

Onboarding Completion RateUse the onboarding dashboard to see how many tasks each new hire has completed and follow up on overdue items.

13 Expense Claims

Go to Expenses. Employees submit claims; managers/HR approve and pay them.

💸 Submitting an Expense Claim (Employee)

1

Click + New Claim.

2

Select Category: travel, meals, office, equipment, or other.

3

Enter Amount (in RM) and a Description.

4

Upload a Receipt (image/file). Click Submit.

17 Tips & Best Practices

1. Set Up Departments & Positions FirstAlways create departments and positions before adding employees — this makes assignment fast and consistent.
2. Assign Roles CarefullyGrant "admin" only to trusted staff. Use "HR" for the HR team and "manager" for team leads so they can approve leave and review their team.
3. Use Shifts for Accurate AttendanceAssign shifts to every employee so the system can automatically detect late arrivals.
4. Approve Leave PromptlyReview pending leave requests daily so employees know their plans are confirmed and payroll deductions are accurate.
5. Process Payroll on TimeGenerate payroll at the start of each month, review, and mark as paid on payday. Keep statutory deductions (EPF, SOCSO, PCB) up to date.
6. Link Recruitment → Onboarding → AssetsWhen you hire someone, immediately create their employee record, set up onboarding tasks, and assign assets — this creates a seamless new-hire experience.
7. Conduct Quarterly Performance ReviewsRegular reviews keep employees engaged and give you a documented record for promotions or salary adjustments.

🚀 Common HR Workflow (Month-End)

  1. Review and approve all pending Leave requests.
  2. Verify Attendance records for the month.
  3. Process Payroll — add overtime, bonuses, deductions.
  4. Review and pay Expense Claims.
  5. Check Performance reviews due this month.
  6. Run Reports for management review.
